# Selah Bible privacy policy

*Last updated: 18 October 2026*

Selah Bible ("Selah") is a Bible reading app for Windows, Android and iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version

Selah does not collect, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your device

Selah saves these things only on your own device, so the app can remember them between visits:

- your highlights, bookmarks and notes
- your reading plan, progress and streaks
- the chapters you have read and where you left off
- your settings, such as the translation, voice and sound

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## When the app goes online

Most of Selah works offline. It connects to the internet only to fetch public content:

- **Narration.** When you tap Listen, the app downloads that chapter's recording and its verse timings from the public Selah project on GitHub (github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com and cdn.jsdelivr.net).

Like any website, these services receive basic technical details needed to deliver a file, such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply to that. Selah does not send them anything about you or what you read.

## Things you choose to do

- **Calendar reminders.** If you tap the calendar link in the reading plan, Google Calendar opens in your browser with a suggested reminder. Nothing is shared unless you save it there.
- **Sharing.** If you share a verse, it goes through your device's own share menu, to the app you pick.
- **Reminders on phones.** Reading reminders are scheduled on your phone itself, not through a server.

## Children

Selah does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
