Where the words come from

The stories are new, simple retellings written for ages 4 to 9 and checked against the Bible passage shown on each one. The memory verses are quoted word for word from the World English Bible, which is in the public domain. The World English Bible uses Yahweh, God’s name in Hebrew, where many Bibles print “the LORD”.

Pictures are drawn in the app. Nothing is downloaded, there are no ads, no accounts, and progress stays on this device.
