# Nature sound credits

The loops in this folder are built by `scripts/nature/build.py` from the edited clips in
[Blanket](https://github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket) (edits by Porrumentzio), shortened, levelled and
crossfaded so they repeat without a seam.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| rain.mp3 | [Rain](https://freesound.org/people/alex36917/sounds/524605/) | alex36917 | CC BY |
| birds.mp3 | [Birds](https://freesound.org/people/kvgarlic/sounds/156826/) | kvgarlic | CC0 |
| stream.mp3 | [Stream](https://freesound.org/people/gluckose/sounds/333987/) | gluckose | CC0 |

Distant thunder is generated in the app while it plays, so it has no file.
